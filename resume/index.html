Where I am now · 2026

So I started building things myself.

At work I decide what gets built and engineers build it. I wanted to understand the last layer too. With AI coding agents I write the specs, set the rules and review every decision, and real products ship. These three are live, and each one taught me something new.

Personal finance app

Libra لیبرا

A Persian, right-to-left cash-flow app on the Jalali calendar. It reads bank SMS into a waiting inbox, tracks budgets, debts and goals, and works as an installable app with notifications.

I wrote the PRD and the specs and set the rules, such as "the admin can never see anyone's amounts". AI agents wrote the code under that plan, with 500+ automated tests.

How I work

Four habits that came with me from every stop.

Start close to the problem.

I talk to the people who live with it, the users and the support team, before I write anything.

Find the system behind it.

Most problems are a process, an incentive or a missing rule. I look for that before proposing a feature.

Decide with evidence, then move.

I weigh value, complexity and effort out loud, pick the simpler option, and keep a way back.

Own it with people.

I move work through clarity and trust: written decisions, honest timelines, and credit where it belongs.
